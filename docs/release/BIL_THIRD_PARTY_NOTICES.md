# BIL third-party notices boundary

BIL includes Flutter/Dart packages declared in `pubspec.yaml` and resolved in
`pubspec.lock`. Their licenses remain the licenses distributed with those
packages and are exposed through Flutter's `LicenseRegistry`; BIL does not
replace or relicense them. The bundled Noto Naskh Arabic font license is kept at
`assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.

The store-art provenance and rights ledger is
`BIL_EPIC15_CONTENT_RIGHTS.json`. Owner-supplied and generated BIL artwork must
retain its SHA-256 evidence. No third-party recipe photo, exercise video,
MyFitnessPal artwork, trademark, or copied store creative is authorized by this
notice.

Before each public release, the resolved dependency graph, bundled font
license, asset-rights audit, and generated store-evidence manifest must all be
rechecked. This file is a notice boundary, not legal advice or a substitute for
the original license texts shipped by dependencies.
