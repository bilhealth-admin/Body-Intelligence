## مسودة وصف المراجعة — لا تُرسل قبل التحقق

BIL Smart Plan adds an owner-bound, reviewable wellness schedule inside the existing BIL interface. A draft becomes active only after the member approves it. Suggested meals are separate from recorded intake, and planned workouts are separate from confirmed activity. Coach writes require validated client receipts. The release candidate also repairs startup retry, bounded cloud recovery and workout-library retry. Physical-device recovery and staged cloud contracts remain pending; this draft is not a claim of completed release certification.
