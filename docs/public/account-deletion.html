Delete your BIL account and data

Developer identity and support contact: OWNER_REQUIRED.

  1. In BIL, open Settings → Account and data → Delete account.
  2. Review the scope, confirm, and follow the status shown in the app.
  3. If you cannot open the app, contact OWNER_REQUIRED from the account email.

Deletion covers the account and developer-controlled profile, health, meal, community, message, media, device-token and cloud records. Data held by Apple Health, Health Connect, an app store, or a destination you selected is controlled separately.

Deleting BIL does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Manage it in the relevant store.
