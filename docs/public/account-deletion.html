Delete your BIL account and data

BIL Health privacy contact: privacy@bilhealth.com. Product support: support@bilhealth.com.

  1. In BIL, open More → Help → Delete account.
  2. Review the deletion scope and the separate app-store billing warning.
  3. Confirm the request and follow the deletion status shown in the app.
  4. If you cannot sign in, contact privacy@bilhealth.com from the account email so the request can be verified.

Eligible developer-controlled account, profile, cloud, Community, message, media, device-token and related records are removed or de-identified by the account-deletion workflow. Data held independently by Apple Health, Health Connect, an app store, or a destination selected by the user is controlled separately.

Deleting BIL does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Manage subscription renewal in the applicable store.

Limited records may be retained only when required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, store accounting, or time-limited backups, as described by the production privacy policy.

Production deletion URL: https://www.bilhealth.com/account-deletion
