# Product Vision

## Vision
Build the world's leading body intelligence platform that helps people understand, predict, and improve their health through scientifically grounded, privacy-first insights.

## Mission
Transform daily body data into understandable, explainable, and actionable decisions.

## North Star
Every recommendation must be:
- Scientifically defensible
- Explainable
- Privacy-first
- Cross-platform
- Maintainable

## BIL Is
- A body intelligence platform
- Offline-first
- Explainable
- User-centric

## BIL Is Not
- A simple calorie counter
- A black-box AI
- A feature collection without strategy
