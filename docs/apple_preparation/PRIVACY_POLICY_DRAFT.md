## Current product behavior

BIL stores profile, body measurements, weight, nutrition, meals, hydration,
preferences, imported health records, and decision evidence locally by default.
Health Connect, HealthKit, and Bluetooth access require platform permission and
user action. Health data is used to display the user's timeline, Body Twin,
explanations, and locally generated decision support.

The current release candidate does not activate accounts, cloud sync, remote
AI, advertising, attribution, analytics, crash reporting, payments,
subscriptions, or developer-controlled upload of health data. An explicit
user-initiated export may send selected content to a destination chosen by the
user through the operating-system share sheet.

Users can revoke platform health or Bluetooth permissions in system settings.
Local application data can be removed through the in-app local-data lifecycle
controls or by uninstalling the application, subject to platform behavior.
Deleting BIL data does not delete source records owned by Health Connect,
HealthKit, another app, or a paired device unless a separately disclosed
operation explicitly says so.

BIL provides tracking and decision support and is not a medical device. It does
not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease and does not replace qualified
medical advice or emergency services.
