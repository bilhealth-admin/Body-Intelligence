## Components and states demonstrated by the references

The screenshots visually demonstrate the following design vocabulary:

- Daily dashboard entry, Quick Add, and exercise-type chooser.
- Explore/My Routines tab shell; only the **Explore** tab content is shown.
- Vertically stacked categories with horizontally swipeable cards.
- Card metadata for duration, equipment level, title, description, and poster art.
- Marketing-style routine detail with hero play control, description, equipment, routine list, and premium CTA.
- Structured logging detail with rounds/AMRAP/to-failure sections, rests, rep/time prescriptions, exercise video thumbnails, and a sticky Log Workout action.
- Locked/blurred premium state.
- Mixed media availability, including placeholder rows where a playable exercise clip is absent.
- Recovery, mobility, bodyweight, dumbbell, kettlebell, HIIT, yoga, breathwork/self-care, and at-home routine families.

## Gaps not proven by the PNG set

The following must not be marked complete from these screenshots alone:

- Actual playback, scrubbing, captions, audio controls, orientation, buffering, retry, download, or offline behavior.
- Workout logging persistence, calorie/volume calculations, history, sync, conflict handling, or duplicate prevention.
- Search, filters, favorites, custom routines, or the contents of the **My Routines** tab.
- Loading, empty, network-error, media-error, and expired-entitlement states other than the one visible locked state.
- RTL, the five BIL locales, dark mode, text scaling, screen-reader semantics, keyboard/focus behavior, or tablet/desktop responsiveness.
- Clinical/safety review of exercise prescriptions, contraindications, coaching cues, or suitability for a specific user.
- Ownership or licensing of any photo, video frame, coach likeness, written routine, external blog reference, partner mark, or marketing copy.
- A count of 100 videos per workout type. The reference set shows example categories and flows, not a licensed inventory count.

## Implemented BIL flow after reference review

- The dashboard, Daily Log, Discover, and Quick Add exercise actions reach the
  production `/wellness/workouts` chooser. Cardio and Strength open the manual
  logger with a real category filter; Workout Routines opens
  `/wellness/workouts/routines`.
- Explore/My Routines, ordered category rails, search, verified-pack
  management, routine details, segment rows, confirmed logging, local save,
  offline/error/empty states, and integrity-checked media are wired to the
  installed-pack boundary.
- Pack `minimumAccess` survives installation and is checked against the same
  server-verified commerce authority at download and presentation. Locked
  routines do not fetch or render their instructions or playback surface and
  show a blurred, accessible membership state instead.
- Six original generated BIL category covers provide distinct Strength,
  Cardio, Mobility, HIIT, Kettlebell, and Recovery fallbacks. Their semantics
  in all five locales state that they are promotional images, not movement
  instructions; provenance and checksums are recorded in
  `BIL_ORIGINAL_MEDIA_PROVENANCE.md`.

These implementation facts still do not satisfy the media activation gate:
the repository contains no licensed 100-video-per-type catalog. The UI remains
honestly empty until a schema-v2 catalog passes rights, count, checksum,
professional safety, entitlement, and real-device playback checks.

## Required media evidence before release

For every BIL workout video or guided-content item, the production catalog needs a machine-checkable rights record containing at least:

1. Stable asset/content identifier and workout taxonomy.
2. Rights source (BIL-produced, commissioned, generated with commercial rights, public domain, or named commercial license).
3. License text/version, territory, platforms, commercial-use permission, modification permission, and expiry if any.
4. Performer/model release and music/voice rights when people or audio are present.
5. Source checksum, transcoded-file checksum, poster checksum, duration, dimensions, codec, and caption/transcript status.
6. Safety reviewer, review date, contraindication notes, and replacement/withdrawal procedure.

Until those records and playable production assets exist, the corresponding rows above remain **reference verified / licensed media outstanding**, not implementation-complete.
