# BIL Terms, Support, and Notices — Owner/Legal Draft

Status: `OWNER_REQUIRED`. This repository draft must not be presented as an
approved legal agreement until the owner supplies legal identity, jurisdiction,
contact, effective date, public HTTPS URLs, and counsel approval.

## Terms of use

BIL provides personal logging, general wellness education and explainable
decision support. It is not a medical device, diagnosis, emergency service,
prescription, or substitute for a qualified clinician. Users remain responsible
for confirming food, serving, device and imported-health records. Store billing
is governed by Apple or Google terms; deleting a BIL account does not cancel a
store subscription. Prohibited use includes abuse, impersonation, unlawful or
dangerous content, unauthorized access, automated scraping, and attempts to
defeat rate limits or security controls.

## Support and safety

Support contact and response schedule: `OWNER_REQUIRED`. Emergency symptoms or
immediate risk must be directed to local emergency services. BIL support must
never request a password, OTP, refresh token, complete health export, or device
secret. Diagnostic sharing is explicit and redacted.

## Community/content policy

Users may post only content they have rights to share. Harassment, hate,
exploitation, sexual content involving minors, dangerous medical claims,
credential theft, spam and unlawful products are prohibited. Report and block
are available. Moderator access is role-controlled and audited; moderators do
not receive arbitrary health records or private-message content outside the
reported safety workflow. Appeal/contact process: `OWNER_REQUIRED`.

## Age suitability and child safety

BIL is not declared child-directed. Minimum age, parental-consent handling and
regional age rules are `OWNER_REQUIRED` and must match store-console answers.
Reports involving child exploitation require immediate preservation/escalation
under applicable law without exposing the material unnecessarily.

## Processors and SDK inventory

Potential processors depend on enabled production features: Supabase (auth,
database, storage, Edge Functions), Apple/Google app stores, APNs/FCM, and the
owner-selected meal-vision gateway. Flutter packages supporting camera, speech,
barcode, sharing, timezone, databases and health/device bridges process data on
device unless their configured runtime explicitly transfers it. The signed
binary and final network trace are authoritative.

## Third-party notices, licenses, and content rights

An automated dependency/license inventory must accompany each release. Package
licenses remain with their authors. USDA and other catalog attribution/licensing
must travel with each content pack. Recipe, exercise, image, video, icon and
translation rights require evidence in the content-rights inventory; absence of
rights blocks publication. Copyright owner/contact and notice process are
`OWNER_REQUIRED`.

## Retention exceptions

Operational data is deleted according to the behavior-derived schedule in the
Epic 12 closure. Exact legal, fraud-prevention, chargeback, moderation and audit
retention periods are `OWNER_REQUIRED`, minimized, access-controlled and stated
in the published privacy policy.
