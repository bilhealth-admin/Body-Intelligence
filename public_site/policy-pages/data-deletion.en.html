Data deletion and privacy choices

Choose the narrowest action that matches what you want: clear local records, disconnect a source, delete cloud data, or delete the full account.

Local device data

Use BIL diary controls to remove individual entries. To clear the remaining local BIL data from a device, remove the app or clear its app storage using the operating system, subject to backups you control.

Cloud-synchronized data

Use More → Settings → Sharing & Privacy to turn encrypted cloud sync off. For full account and associated cloud-data deletion, use More → Help → Delete Account. For a narrower verified privacy request, email privacy@bilhealth.com from the account address.

HealthKit, Health Connect, and devices

Disconnect the integration in BIL and revoke permission in system settings. Source records remain in the source platform unless you delete them there. BIL cannot delete data owned by another app or hardware provider.

AI, voice, and photos

Disable remote AI consent to stop new requests. Delete supported conversation history in BIL. Voice and meal-photo processing occurs only after your action; provider retention is governed by BIL configuration and the processor agreement.

Advertising choices

Paid plans do not display ads. Free-tier ads, when production configuration and required consent are present, are contextual/non-personalized and must not receive health records, diary content, AI prompts, or body measurements.

Request help

Email privacy@bilhealth.com from your account email. Describe the category you want removed, but never attach sensitive exports. BIL will verify the request and explain any lawful limitation.
