Contact support

Email support@bilhealth.com. Include your platform, app version, language, and a concise description. Never send a password, one-time code, access token, full purchase token, identity document, or raw health export.

Account and sign-in

Use the in-app password reset flow and the same verified email used to create the account. If a reset link is expired, request a new one and use only the newest message.

Purchases and restore

Confirm you are signed into the same Apple or Google store account, then open BIL Plans and choose Restore Purchases. Store product prices are unavailable on emulators that do not provide real storefront metadata.

Connected health and devices

Check system permission, Bluetooth state, device compatibility, and the source shown in BIL. BIL never claims a device is connected when the platform has not verified it.

Privacy, export, and deletion

Use More → Settings → Sharing & Privacy for consent, encrypted cloud sync, and data export. Use More → Help → Delete Account for full account deletion. For a verified privacy request, email privacy@bilhealth.com from the account address.

Security and community safety

Report suspected account compromise, harmful content, or a security issue to bilhealth.app@gmail.com. Do not test against other users or include real health data in a vulnerability report.
