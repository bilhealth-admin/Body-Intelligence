COMMUNITY SAFETY

Community guidelines

BIL community spaces are for respectful, adult health and fitness support. This policy explains the rules for every community interaction, how to report harm, and how BIL uses human moderation.

Scope and acceptance

These rules apply to profiles, circles and their images, posts, comments, replies, likes and other reactions, friend requests, friendships, private messages, reports, and community food contributions. Accepting community-policy-v1 records your agreement to this version. If you do not accept, you may still use available account, privacy, reporting, and blocking controls, but you cannot publish or interact on surfaces that require acceptance. A new policy version requires a new acceptance.

Adults only

BIL and its community features are for people aged 18 or older. Do not create an account for a minor, solicit contact with minors, or post content that depicts or sexualizes minors.

Respect and safety

Do not harass, threaten, shame, stalk, discriminate, impersonate, exploit, sexually solicit, or disclose another person’s private information. Never encourage self-harm, eating-disorder behavior, dangerous restriction, unsafe exercise, violence, or illegal activity. Do not evade a block or suspension by using another account.

Likes, replies, and connections

Use comments, replies, and likes to support honest, relevant discussion. Do not coordinate harassment, manipulate reactions or engagement, create fake support, or use likes as pressure. Send friend requests only for genuine BIL wellness interaction, respect an ignored or declined request, and never repeatedly contact or pursue someone who has not welcomed it.

Wellness—not medical treatment

Community content reflects personal experiences and must not be presented as diagnosis or treatment. Do not claim guaranteed cures, prescribe or direct medication use, market unapproved medical products, encourage extreme dieting or unsafe training, or tell someone to ignore qualified care or emergency services.

Content you may share

Share only content you created or have permission to use. Do not post spam, scams, deceptive promotions, malware, explicit sexual content, graphic violence, hateful content, copyrighted material without authorization, or health records and identifiers belonging to another person.

Keep contact inside BIL

Do not share or request email addresses, phone numbers, URLs or domains, social-media handles, or other contact details, and do not ask anyone to move a conversation outside BIL. BIL may automatically reject content containing those details or an off-platform invitation. Obscuring or spacing contact details to evade this protection is also prohibited.

Report, block, and urgent risk

Use the in-app report and block controls for content, messages, or member behavior that may break these rules. Blocking is a safety boundary and must not be evaded. You may also email support@bilhealth.com with the account or content reference and a concise explanation. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or raw health exports. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services; BIL is not an emergency channel.

Circle creation and review

Eligible signed-in members may submit a circle after accepting the Community Guidelines and saving a community display name and BIL code. New circles and newly submitted circle images remain visible only to their creator and authorized reviewers until approved. Uploading an image is not approval; a previously approved image remains in use while a replacement is reviewed. An independent authorized moderator may approve or reject a submission, or remove a reported circle. Circle creators do not approve their own submissions. Use the circle report action or support to report a concern.

Human moderation and enforcement

Authorized human moderators review submitted posts and reports. Automated safety filters may prevent clearly restricted content from being submitted, but they support rather than replace human judgment. BIL may hold or limit visibility, reject or remove content, suspend community features, or restrict accounts to enforce these rules, protect users, comply with law, or investigate abuse. Review decisions consider context, severity, repetition, and credible risk. Where appropriate, users may request another review through support.

Privacy and health information

Community content may be visible to the audience selected in the app. Private messages are access-controlled but are not end-to-end encrypted. Share only the minimum information needed for a supportive conversation; never publish another person’s health data, identity, or location, and avoid posting your own sensitive health information. Privacy or deletion requests may be sent to privacy@bilhealth.com.
